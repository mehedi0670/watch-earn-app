EarnZA privacy notice

EarnZA stores the name, email, device identifier, emulator or root signals, points balance, ad-view records, referral code, and cash-out details needed to run the rewards programme. Cash-out details can include a South African mobile number or bank account, and later a 1Voucher PIN or payment reference.

That information is used to sign you in, keep one account on a phone, credit views that Google has verified, and pay a cash out that a person has approved. It is not sold.

Google may process data for AdMob and, once Firebase is switched on, for sign-in. You can delete the account in the Android app under Profile. Bank numbers and voucher PINs are removed. A record of the amounts may be kept for accounting.
